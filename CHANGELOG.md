# Changelog

## 0.2.1

- Fixed facade faces disappearing where a cable joins them when playing with Sodium and MoreCulling.

## 0.2.0

- Frames and looks can now be put on a cable by right-clicking the face of a neighbouring block, aiming into the cable's spot, just like placing a block there.
- Fixed facades having flat, stepped lighting instead of smooth lighting.

## 0.1.1

- Fixed the side of a facade being invisible where the cable inside connects to another cable.

## 0.1.0

- Initial release: Cloak Frames for OmniVault cables. Put a frame on a cable, then right-click it with any block to make the cable look like that block.
